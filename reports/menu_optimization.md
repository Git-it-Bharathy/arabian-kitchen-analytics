# Arabian Kitchen — Menu Optimization Recommendations

Classification based on menu engineering: popularity (units sold) vs estimated contribution margin per unit, split on each metric's average across the menu.

- Average units sold per item: **6,933**
- Average estimated margin per unit: **₹109**

- Consider dropping or reworking **Manakish Zaatar** — lowest combination of popularity and margin.
- Push **Mixed Grill Platter** harder — highest margin among underordered items; a small menu placement or staff-recommendation change could lift revenue with minimal cost.
